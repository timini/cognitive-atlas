## Scientific finding and honest verdict

Interesting exploratory evidence about accuracy, error arrangement and elicitation dependence, but the current paper is not yet a strong cognitive-theory submission. More model families improve generality; independently blocked, reviewed prompt variants and held-out cross-task prediction add more explanatory value.

1. **There is a useful empirical result, but its novelty must be precise.** The high rank correlations establish that outputs track geography; they do not establish a common reusable representation. Accurate estimates can still have inconsistent error arrangements. The observed/control triangle rates (English10.69/11.52%, Arabic13.30/12.30%, Chinese10.74/11.85%) show why the raw violation percentage is weak evidence on its own. The direction of the signed-error pattern is potentially worth investigating. It is not yet a cognitive mechanism, a replicated language effect or a population result.
2. **The current statistical wording is substantially more defensible than a strong cognitive-map claim would be.** The sign-control code preserves every absolute pair error and its admissible domain; it does not preserve signed bias or cross-pair dependence. Its control percentiles are artificial-assignment summaries. The paper correctly distinguishes these from confidence intervals. An inferential rejection cannot be retrofitted onto a descriptive control without a justified model and calibrated procedure.
3. **The 100 capitals, 4,950 pairs, 161,700 triples and 148,500 completed slots are different units.** Overlapping triples share edges and pairs share capitals; these counts must not be treated as independent cognitive replications. The existing fixed-pair analysis does not automatically become invalid because capitals recur: the issue is the particular independence/exchangeability assumptions used for response assignments and any intended generalization. The paper correctly admits that dispatches clustered within roughly one second do not establish those assumptions. Replicate across independent collection blocks, prompts and systems if those are the target units.
4. **Language remains confounded with wording.** English asks for a great-circle distance; Arabic and Chinese add a shortest-surface explanation, and all retain English entity names. The response-distribution permutation can reject for dispersion changes even with equal population medians. The fitted-map statistic does not change that null. Regional gains are descriptive and negative in both operational groups; the current corrected manuscript does not claim a local advantage or valid regional significance. Ten observations do not identify tails, modality or epistemic confidence. No normal or purely random sampling law has been established.
5. **The reconstruction has not been tested as a representation used in another task.** Sphere controls and multiple starts address geometry and optimization; they do not establish uniqueness, mechanism or held-out validity. Numeric geometry could predict proximity answers simply because both approximate real geography. Compare its held-out predictive value against true-geography, individual-pair and specified heuristic baselines; prespecify informative disagreements among them. Keep directional and ordinal tasks distinct, and count their observations separately. Evidence of such transfer would support a stronger behavioral representation claim, still not a literal neural map.
6. **The paper needs a positive cognitive argument, not fewer caveats.** Its operational definition is sensible, but the abstract and conclusion identify a benchmark, and the Introduction does not develop rival predictions. A theoretical account should explain which pattern distinguishes common relational knowledge from independent retrieval, numeric calibration or region-based heuristics. Psychogeographic experience, human biases and cultural mechanisms are not measured here. Human comparisons or activation work are optional routes tied to particular claims, not universal publication prerequisites.
7. **Add model families, but fix the design at the same time.** A prospective comparison including the existing provider and at least two independently trained/provider families would test scope more directly than another version of the same family. Prespecify model selection and distinguish provider from model, version and sampling settings. Use native-reviewed semantically matched prompt variants, randomized order and independent time blocks; treat localized names as a separate factor. Start with a powered or simulation-justified diagnostic design rather than multiplying every dimension indiscriminately. No new collection was performed or authorized by this review.
