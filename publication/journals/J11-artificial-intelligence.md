# J11 — Artificial Intelligence (AIJ, Elsevier)

Checked 9 September 2026. **Fit judgment: ambitious, lower-priority target for the current paper.** A general AI journal is likely to demand a clearer advance in evaluation or understanding than a single-model descriptive case study currently demonstrates. Recommend a regular research article if pursuing it; a Research Note would require a much tighter, genuinely focused result. This is a judgment about the current 100-capital manuscript, not an inferred acceptance probability.

**Evidence caveat:** The current ScienceDirect Guide for Authors and aims page returned access errors. The journal's own IJCAI editorial site supplies directly verified initial-submission instructions, while applicable Elsevier policies cover ethics and AI. Precise unverified production limits are deliberately not invented. Its editorial pages are dated 2021 and should be reconciled with the live submission portal before submission.

**Labels:** V = verified rule; G = editorial guidance; R = our recommendation; U = unresolved.

| Criterion | Submission criterion and practical style guidance |
|---|---|
| C01 Scope | **G:** Broad AI, including cognition, reasoning, knowledge representation and language processing [S1]. **R:** Center a general diagnostic question about the consistency of learned-system judgments, rather than the engineering of a world-map website. |
| C02 Contribution/article type | **G:** Mature, complete and novel work; regular articles and Research Notes [S2]. **R:** Regular article best matches the multiple analyses. The methods are established tools; argue for the empirical insight and avoid manufacturing an algorithmic novelty claim. |
| C03 Word/page/abstract limits | **G:** No hard article length cap in the editorial FAQ; 20–30 standard pages advised. Research Notes maximum 4,500 words [S2]. **U:** Current publisher abstract limit unverified. **R:** Do not pad the current article toward that advisory length. |
| C04 Structure/title/keywords | **V:** English, abstract and references required [S3]. **U:** Current mandatory keywords/highlights counts unverified. **R:** Prepare a concise abstract and optional highlights draft, but validate portal requirements before uploading. Main findings should identify the model and fixed prompt design. |
| C05 Files/LaTeX/template | **G:** A4/Letter, single column, at least 11-point preferred; avoid small double-column conference formatting. `elsarticle` recommended [S3]. **Final:** Additional publisher requirements apply after acceptance [S3]. **R:** Change the existing 10-point class and retain editable equations, tables and vector figures. |
| C06 Citations/references | **U:** Current publisher citation style not verified. **R:** Use the official `elsarticle` configuration once the guide is available. Preserve the documented distinction from prior city-distance reconstruction and cite the actual geodesic and optimization implementations used. |
| C07 Figures/tables/supplement | **V:** Submission figures should approach publication quality [S3]. **U:** Current artwork/supplement ceilings unverified. **R:** Embed legible static plots, explain what alignment changes, and provide source figures and a reproduction archive separately when the portal supports it. |
| C08 Data/code/reproducibility | **U:** AIJ's current journal-specific data-policy tier was inaccessible. **R:** Include a data/code availability statement, immutable observation identifiers, exact prompt texts and versioned analysis. Do not convert a publisher-wide encouragement into an unverified journal mandate. |
| C09 Methods/statistical reporting | **G:** Complete evidence and literature context matter [S2]. **R:** Explain the scientific value of the reference-sphere control, finite-sample diagnostics and permutation null. Report language/wording confounding and failed rare-tail calibration before drawing substantive conclusions about linguistic representation. |
| C10 Human authorship/ethics/funding/conflicts | **V:** Elsevier's publishing-ethics policy applies [S4]. **R:** Prepare authentic contribution, funding, conflict and accountability statements. Independently verify statistical implementation and references; automated reviews are supporting tools, not substitutes for responsible human authors. |
| C11 Generative-AI policy/disclosure | **V:** Applicable June 2026 Elsevier policy permits supportive AI with human oversight; disclose manuscript preparation before references and research/code assistance in Methods; no AI authors [S5]. **R:** Name the tools and actual purposes. Distinguish elicited model observations from assistant support used to build and write the study. |
| C12 Peer review/anonymity | **U:** AIJ's current anonymity model was not directly verified. **R:** Prepare both author metadata and a removable identifying layer; check the journal portal before deciding whether to anonymize. Elsevier's generic double-anonymous guidance does not prove AIJ uses that process. |
| C13 Fees/license/access | **U:** Current AIJ APC, subscription-route charges and available licenses were not directly verified; no numeric budget asserted. **R:** Inspect the current journal publishing-options page and institutional agreement. Do not use a third-party cached author pack as a current fee quote. |
| C14 Submission package/exclusivity/prior publication | **V:** Use Editorial Manager [S2, S3]; Elsevier ethics prohibits concurrent journal consideration [S4]. **G:** Conference-derived work may be eligible when complete and sufficiently novel [S2]. **R:** Disclose preprint and prior-publication history, verify all mandatory files, and obtain all authors' final approval. |
