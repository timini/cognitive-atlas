## Editorial tone and scientific bar — assessment, not publisher rules

A research article would be the sensible route if confirmed by the live guide. Lead with whether relational spatial judgments express a coherent, reusable configuration. Define the operational meaning of cognitive map before the metrics. Present geodesic accuracy as a validation anchor. The central theoretical contrast is a transferable relational representation versus a collection of individually retrieved facts or task-specific heuristics.

The current manuscript is careful and reproducible, but its opening identifies a benchmark more clearly than a cognitive problem. Add a prediction-bearing account and a task-transfer or held-out test before claiming representational organization. A triplet task derived from the fitted numeric geometry could be especially informative. Multiple model families would test scope, but extra vendor columns alone would leave the mechanism question unresolved.

Use psychogeography as a carefully defined motivation if desired; this experiment measures numeric judgments, not affective experience of places. No journal rule requiring human participants was verified. A human comparison could strengthen particular claims but must not be invented as a universal submission prerequisite.
