## Fit and intellectual tone — editorial assessment

This is a strong intellectual destination for the intended project, but an ambitious submission for the present evidence. Lead with the organization and context sensitivity of spatial knowledge expressed by an intelligent system. Geography supplies a known relational structure against which competing accounts can be evaluated. Explain what reconstruction contributes beyond a collection of correct facts: for example, whether an inferred configuration predicts unqueried relational judgments, and whether distortions survive changes in elicitation.

The current abstract ends by announcing a benchmark; the introduction spends more space locating geographic evaluations than developing a cognitive problem. Renaming this a cognitive map will not resolve that weakness. The most promising observation is the separation between aggregate accuracy and the arrangement of signed errors; the current error-sign control is descriptive and does not discriminate retrieval, heuristics, and spatial computation. A preregistered task-transfer contrast and independent model-family replication would make a stronger contribution than adding another accuracy leaderboard.

Do not claim a literal internal map, human-like experience, or cultural representations from these API observations. Equally, the absence of activation measurements does not rule out a behavioral cognitive-science contribution. No universal requirement for new human participants was found; recommending a human comparison is a theory-validation judgment, not a submission rule.

## Verified journal-specific rules

The [detailed author guide](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/15516709/homepage/forauthors.html) prioritizes multidisciplinary cognitive work, including computational accounts of human or nonhuman processes. Narrow refinements receive low priority. Approximate upper bounds excluding references are 12,000 words (regular), 18,000 (extended), and 4,000 (brief). Abstract: 150–250 words; keywords: 4–8. Sections are numbered. Initial submission is free format; APA is the eventual reference style. Word or LaTeX is supported, with compiled PDF for review. Review is double anonymized: separate title page and anonymized materials. Its open-materials policy requires review-accessible materials and reproducibility resources before acceptance, subject to justified exceptions. Preprints are permitted. A concise cover letter should explain multidisciplinary fit and author approval; extended length needs justification. No submission fee is stated; optional open access has an APC. No current APC amount is asserted here.

The [alternate guide](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/15516709/author-guidelines) requires a data-availability statement, submitting-author ORCID, CRediT, and disclosure of substantive AI assistance in Methods. It specifies separate supporting files, editable LaTeX plus PDF and all dependencies. Human authors must approve and take responsibility for the submission. Participant ethics statements apply where relevant. This page is labeled **Under Review** and contains internally inconsistent hybrid/open-access wording; it also calls the cover letter optional, unlike the detailed guide. Prepare the stricter package and verify these discrepancies before submission rather than treating either conflict as settled.
