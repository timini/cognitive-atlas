## Adaptation for this manuscript — editorial judgment

The present 15-page author layout is not the publisher’s typeset-page count. Count the actual main text and move the capital inventory, protocol tables, bootstrap derivation and extended sensitivities into a separate supplement before judging length. Keep the four central figures and choose no more than four main tables. The approximately 194-word abstract is near the stated 200-word boundary and should be checked after conversion. Its general-science readability matters more than fitting one more metric into it. Complete the human author and AI-preparation record; the current paragraph about AI reviews does not document AI drafting or coding.
