# Broad-science and research-data source audit

Checked 9 September 2026. This note records access and interpretation for J13–J18; criterion-level citations are in the individual guides. Only official publisher/journal material supports submission rules. Fit and priority are editorial judgments.

## PLOS ONE (J13)

Read the journal's [submission guide](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/submission-guidelines), [getting-started page](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/getting-started), [publication criteria](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/criteria-for-publication), [data policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/data-availability), [ethical-publishing/AI policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/ethical-publishing-practice), and [publisher fee page](https://plos.org/fees/). The format-free initial-submission statement is explicit, but the separate [LaTeX page](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/latex) warns of template-related returns and asks for separate figures even initially. Both instructions are recorded; confirm the route-specific portal treatment rather than claiming an unconditional format-free pass. The fees page's graphical journal labels are missing from extracted text; its first card covers the PLOS ONE protocol and other-article categories. Confirm the journal/card association again before invoicing. No acceptance-rate claim is made.

## Scientific Reports (J14)

The [full submission guide](https://www.nature.com/srep/author-instructions/submission-guidelines) and [editorial policies](https://www.nature.com/srep/journal-policies/editorial-policies) were readable in official indexed content. Direct requests to the dedicated AI, checklist and attempted fee pages returned cookie-only content; HTTP200 alone was not counted as a verified rule. The main guide supports the LLM disclosure/authorship statement, but does not substitute for checking the entire current AI policy. The [portfolio OA page](https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/for-authors/publish-with-us/open-access) lists Scientific Reports from €2,490. This is not a journal-specific guaranteed quote. Word/page advice and numerical display limits must be separated; manuscript author-layout pages are not final typeset pages.

## Royal Society Open Science (J15)

Read [2026 catalogue](https://royalsociety.org/-/media/journals/librarians/catalogue/2026-royal-society-journals-catalogue.pdf), [author guide](https://www.royalsociety.org/journals/authors/author-guidelines/), [AI policy](https://www.royalsociety.org/journals/ethics-policies/artificial-intelligence/), [open-science policy](https://royalsociety.org/journals/open-access/open-science/) and [fee page](https://royalsociety.org/journals/open-access/). The author guide supports format-free initial submission. The AI policy limits writing assistance to readability/language and reserves core scientific responsibilities for humans: disclosure alone does not establish eligibility for this preparation history. Its AI-topic exception is explicitly for articles without AI-generated content. The eight-journal Subscribe to Open change is not a no-fee policy for this gold-OA journal. A 2022 author-journey sheet said 3–6 keywords while the current author guide says 3–10; use the current guide and verify the live form.

## PeerJ Computer Science (J16)

The [author instructions](https://peerj.com/about/author-instructions/), [policies](https://peerj.com/about/policies-and-procedures/) and [criteria](https://peerj.com/about/editorial-criteria/) returned403 through the search browser but were successfully retrieved directly over HTTPS. Read the actual content, including the GenAI section. PeerJ explicitly links Taylor & Francis's AI policy and guidance. It supports data visualization assistance but retains human responsibility and concerns about extensive AI contribution; draft-generation eligibility must not be inferred from copyediting permission. Its author guide requires DOI archiving for software on social-coding platforms and gives a30-MB individual supplement limit. The [pricing page](https://peerj.com/pricing/) yielded only a JavaScript placeholder; no current fee amount was verified. Temporary retrieved HTML was used for inspection and is not republished here.

## EPJ Data Science (J17)

Read [scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/13688/aims-and-scope), [submission guide](https://link.springer.com/journal/13688/submission-guidelines), [Regular Article instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/13688/submission-guidelines/regular-article), and linked [Springer policies](https://link.springer.com/brands/springer/journal-policies). Current scope names generative language models, but still requires substantive human/social-system insight and cautions against purely descriptive standard-method applications. The article page supplies abstract/declaration requirements. The guide gives a current acceptance-date APC and allows different Creative Commons license choices. A publicly downloadable bundle does not by itself satisfy the guide's recognized-repository, software-license and archive-information requirements.

## Data Science Journal (J18)

Read [about](https://datascience.codata.org/about), [author guide](https://datascience.codata.org/about/submissions), and [editorial policies](https://datascience.codata.org/about/editorialpolicies). The about page gives the8,000-word research-article ceiling; the author guide says references count. Its DOI rule is explicit. Two current fee values coexist on the same page: £790 in the table and £770 in prose. Treat the price as unresolved, with applicable taxes extra. The direct [Ubiquity AI endpoint](https://ubiquitypress.com/ai-policy) was inaccessible, but the official [older policy route](https://ubiquitypress.com/site/AI-policy/) was indexed with last-updated7November2024. That version allows declared manuscript preparation while using broad language against AI-created original research data; the empirical collection of LLM outputs needs explanation and clarification, not an invented exemption. Actual journal reviewers may not use GenAI to create their reports. These author-commissioned internal assessments are not submitted as journal peer-review reports.

## Coverage limitations

No journal portal account was created or submission begun. Missing or conflicting instructions are identified in the guides. No institutional affiliation, funding coverage, author identity, ethics decision or license consent is assumed. Before actual submission, a human corresponding author must recheck the selected journal's current rules and confirm unresolved points. This is a practical shortlist with exclusions/holds, not a claim that all18 journals will accept or even send this manuscript to review.
