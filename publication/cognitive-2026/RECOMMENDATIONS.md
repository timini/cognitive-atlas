# Review synthesis and publication decision

This assessment concerns the current 100-capital paper at `56fa5700fd65d7c0b911af1cce47debdbce62232`. The new journal strategy treats the project as behavioral research on spatial knowledge expressed through language. The existing geographic measurements provide validation, rather than determining the intellectual audience.

## Editorial judgment

**There is an interesting exploratory study here. The current version is not yet a strong explanatory cognitive-science submission.** It describes global order, calibration, collective inconsistency and prompt-condition variation in considerable detail. It does not yet show which aspects form a stable, reusable organization of judgments, or distinguish that possibility from task-specific retrieval and numerical heuristics.

The response count is not the chief limitation. One model alias, one collection period and one instruction wording per language cannot support broad claims about model families or language-specific cognitive worlds. More systems would broaden the evidence, but the same numeric task repeated across brands would leave the explanatory gap largely intact.

The most useful next step is an independently elicited comparative-proximity task, predicted from a geometry fitted only to training judgments, with simple reference and calibration baselines. Begin with a diagnostic subset drawn from the same 100-capital pool before paying for another full sweep. Add matched model-family replication, independently reviewed equivalent translations, paraphrases and time blocks. The [research assessment](RESEARCH-ASSESSMENT.md) gives discriminating predictions, limitations and call-budget examples. These are proposed studies, not newly measured results or a guarantee of sufficient statistical power.

## Journal priorities

1. **Cognitive Processing:** the most practical initial intellectual target. Its scope explicitly includes artificial cognition. Develop the theory and validate cross-task or cross-occasion regularity before preparing the final anonymous package.
2. **Cognitive Science:** the aspirational target with the closest connection to language-derived spatial knowledge. It needs a contribution of multidisciplinary cognitive interest, stronger than extending a geographic benchmark.
3. **Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications:** plausible if the empirical cognitive question is motivated by a real practical problem and the Significance Statement follows from tested evidence.

Spatial Cognition & Computation is a strong conceptual match, but current AI manuscript-drafting policy conflicts with this project's preparation history; its eligibility needs clarification. It should not silently replace one scope mismatch with an unresolved submission-policy problem. Topics in Cognitive Science requires an accepted thematic route. Cognition, Cognitive Psychology and Psychonomic Bulletin & Review are ambitious later targets. Memory & Cognition, Frontiers Psychology—Cognition and Journal of Environmental Psychology require a substantive connection to human cognition or human–environment relations. Behavior Research Methods would require a validated measurement-method contribution.

These distinctions concern both fit and readiness. A journal with a compatible scope is not automatically an easy publication. No acceptance probability or aggregate compliance score is offered.

## What can be fixed with the existing data

| Priority | Revision | Completion condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rebuild the introduction around relational knowledge, coherence and elicitation context. | It states competing behavioral predictions and explains why each measured quantity bears on them; it does not suggest the present design already distinguishes all accounts. |
| 2 | Explain the positive observation before its boundaries. | The reader can state the supported finding without having to infer it from a catalogue of limitations. |
| 3 | Reorganize figures around the cognitive argument. | Accuracy, structure and reliability have clear conceptual roles; warped coastlines are marked as interpolation rather than independently elicited shapes. |
| 4 | Preserve the corrected statistics. | The sign control remains descriptive; response-distribution tests do not become median-map tests; regional findings do not become a local-language advantage. |
| 5 | Separate the article from its execution audit. | Move long entity listings and low-level provenance to supplements while keeping design, denominators, assumptions and inference-changing failures in the main text. |
| 6 | Prepare one venue's package. | Match its verified abstract, references, anonymity, supplementary and disclosure rules. Do not combine twelve incompatible templates. |

These changes can make an honest exploratory paper more compelling. They cannot retrospectively validate translations, create independent sampling periods or turn a post hoc contrast into a preregistered test.

## What needs new evidence

| Question | Most informative addition | Claim it could strengthen |
|---|---|---|
| Is the inferred organization reusable? | Fresh comparative judgments and held-out prediction from the numeric geometry. | Cross-task predictive organization of expressed spatial knowledge. |
| Is it reproducible beyond one elicitation occasion? | Randomized matched time blocks, reviewed wording variants and increased repeats on a prespecified subset. | Stability across the sampled occasions and formulations. |
| Is it specific to one system? | Two additional model families, with the reference system recollected under the same new protocol. | Generality across those named systems; not all LLMs. |
| Is the difficulty numerical or relational? | Training-only monotone calibration and an independently elicited ordinal task. | Separation of calibration error from ordering performance. |
| Is there a meaningful human analogue? | A matched human task or an explicit cognitive model with held-out predictions. | The specified human-cognition comparison, subject to appropriate design and ethics. |

A human experiment is not a universal prerequisite for computational cognitive science. It becomes especially valuable when the intended claim concerns human-like distortions, familiarity or subjective geography. Mechanistic access is similarly optional for behavioral claims and necessary only for particular internal-process claims.

## Submission facts still needed

Human authors must supply and validate authorship, contributions, funding, conflicts and scientific accountability, and decide reuse rights and archival arrangements. These are separate from whether the research is interesting. The substantial AI role in code, translation, analysis, drafting and review must be disclosed accurately; cosmetic rewriting cannot alter the history.

The five reports are author-commissioned AI assessments, not independent human peer review. Their agreement is useful for identifying common weaknesses but cannot substitute for subject-expert evaluation or a journal decision. The full report-level findings and differing emphases remain in the [five reviews](README.md#five-fresh-reviews).
