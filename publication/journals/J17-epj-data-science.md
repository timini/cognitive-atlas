## Fit and proposed route

**Stretch candidate requiring a stronger substantive contribution.** Proposed type: Regular Article. Generative language models are explicitly in scope, but the journal asks for insight into human/social systems, not only descriptive use of standard methods. This paper should not infer cultural mechanisms merely to meet that threshold.

## Adaptation for this manuscript — editorial judgment

Do not turn the present conditional observations into unsupported claims about cultures or societies. A defensible route would explain what this benchmark establishes about socially produced language models, ideally with independent prompt/date/model replication as a substantive extension. Without that contribution it risks desk rejection on scope and depth, even if the technical checks pass. The current public research package needs a recognized repository, reuse license and file-size-aware supplement plan. Formal declaration headings and source-file style are straightforward later adaptations; they will not solve the contribution gap.
