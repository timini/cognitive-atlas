# Submission plan for the 100-capital paper

**Prepare Transactions in GIS first, with PLOS ONE and Scientific Reports as practical alternatives.** TMLR is the strongest additional AI-facing option if its policy on substantial AI assistance is clarified for this preparation history. This is an editorial recommendation based on scope and preparation requirements, not a forecast of acceptance.

The assessment concerns the [frozen 100-capital manuscript](https://github.com/timini/cognitive-atlas/tree/5f9bdaa3641f684708ad914a721eecc25835ab64/paper), one model alias, and English, Arabic and written-Chinese instruction conditions. The journal guides use official sources checked on 9 September 2026. This dossier does not modify the paper or collect new observations. The five reports are separately prepared AI-assisted assessments, not independent human referee opinions or journal decisions.

## What the paper can contribute

The strongest contribution is the measured relationship between **accurate distance ordering and imperfect geometric consistency**. Repeated judgments, spherical reconstruction, and the Earth reference control allow the paper to distinguish these properties. The review reports recommend explaining that distinction before listing methods and metrics. The size of the collection supports the analysis; its storage format and the existence of a website do not establish scientific novelty.

The current observations can support a bounded descriptive paper. A general claim about language, cultural familiarity, hidden neural representations or different model families would require evidence beyond this design. The three conditions differ in wording as well as language; they use English place names, one alias and one collection period. Resampling the existing responses cannot create independent replications of those factors.

## Revisions possible with the existing observations

1. **Make the inferential boundary visible at each main claim.** Report shuffled-baseline results conditional on the assumed within-pair exchangeability model. Rejection of equal response distributions does not establish unequal population median maps; different response dispersion can reject that null. Keep the separate multiplicity families explicit.
2. **Reduce the regional significance emphasis.** Retain observed gains, interactions, group definitions and sensitivities. The rare-tail calibration failure limits what the displayed empirical-bootstrap intervals and p-values establish. Moving probability columns to a sensitivity supplement is an editorial recommendation, not a journal-imposed test. Keep the failure and its implications visible in reviewed text. Do not turn an observed lack of gain into proof that local benefits are absent.
3. **Simplify the narrative and abstract.** Lead with the research question, the accuracy–coherence result and its scope. Explain which estimand each number describes. Distinguish completed responses from valid numerical values, fixed-condition comparisons from language effects, and capital reconstruction from illustrative coastline deformation. Remove revision-process language from the eventual submission version.
4. **Finish a durable research release.** The repository and reproduction ZIP are publicly accessible. Update the frozen paper’s stale private-repository statement in a future revision. Choose appropriate code/data licenses through the actual rights holders and deposit a version in an accepted archive. Access, an integrity hash, a reuse license and a DOI each serve different purposes; some venues require a DOI while others recommend it.
5. **Prepare the selected venue’s files.** Reflow the paper in the required template, check the relevant word-count convention, references, figures, supplements and anonymity. The 41.8-MB ZIP exceeds PeerJ’s and EPJ’s stated single-file supplement limits; an accepted repository or properly split supplement can preserve all observations. Do not apply 18 incompatible templates to one manuscript.

These actions should preserve the existing data and documented historical limitations. The paper does not need new model calls merely to shorten an abstract or present a narrower conclusion. New collection would be needed for stronger claims across phrasings, languages, time blocks, model versions or entity-name conventions. Those are a separate replication study, not measurements already obtained.

## Facts that the actual authors must supply

The blank author field, contribution record and absent funding/conflict declarations cannot be completed by inference from a GitHub account. The responsible people must establish qualifying authorship, validate the scientific claims, approve the submission and state the applicable facts. The AI-use record should distinguish Gemini as the measured system from assistance used in drafting, coding, analysis, translation, figures and reviews. Tool/version details and human checks should be described as far as the evidence supports them.

This is unfinished submission information, not a finding that funding, conflicts or misconduct exist. No identities, approvals, rights or editorial permissions have been invented in this dossier.

The [editorial review](reviews/review-01-editorial.md) prioritizes the publishable result and the submission sequence. The [methods review](reviews/review-02-methods.md) examines the inferential target, dependence assumptions, uncertainty calibration and denominators. The [contribution review](reviews/review-03-contribution.md) assesses novelty, geographic interpretation and audience fit. The [reproducibility review](reviews/review-04-reproducibility.md) independently extracts the public release and checks its contracts and six paper-specific tests. The [style and policy review](reviews/review-05-style-policy.md) focuses on writing and the venue-specific preparation requirements. Each still assesses all 18 journals against all 14 criteria.

Reviewers may assign different category ratings: a category can contain a verified format rule, an unresolved policy and missing author information at once. Read the explanation rather than averaging Pass/Partial/Fail labels into a score. The reports consistently distinguish measured effects from broader claims; they differ in the relative priority of specialist venues and the severity assigned to incomplete categories. This synthesis preserves those judgments rather than treating agreement among AI agents as independent validation.
