# J07 — Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics

Checked 9 September 2026 against official sources. **Fit judgment: plausible but demanding specialist target.** The strongest pitch is a reproducible evaluation of elicited multilingual spatial judgments. The contribution must explain what NLP researchers learn beyond earlier city-distance MDS studies. One model, one wording per language, and no independently validated translations constrain the claim. Recommended type: original empirical/resources-and-evaluation paper. This is a preparation guide, not an eligibility or acceptance guarantee.

**Labels:** V = verified requirement; G = journal guidance; R = our recommendation; U = unresolved. Initial-submission requirements apply unless explicitly marked final-publication.

| Criterion | Submission criterion and practical style guidance |
|---|---|
| C01 Scope | **V:** Computational linguistics/NLP, including evaluation and resources [S1]. **R:** Explain why the geometry probes language-model behavior rather than just supplying an attractive geography demonstration. |
| C02 Contribution/article type | **V:** Completed, original work; technical strength, correctness, significance and relevance matter [S1]. **R:** Present the fixed 100-capital benchmark as a focused empirical contribution. Do not advertise a new embedding algorithm or universal language effect. |
| C03 Word/page/abstract limits | **V:** Ten main-content pages for original submissions; separate appendix allowances are five replication-detail pages and three complementary-result pages [S3]. **U:** No independent abstract word ceiling verified. **R:** Reflow in the actual template before assessing length; the current 15-page generic article is not a TACL page count. |
| C04 Structure/title/keywords | **G:** Five to ten keywords are optional in editorial comments [S2]. **R:** Keep the title literal; define “cognitive” operationally. Put denominators, uncertainty qualifications and primary evidence in the main text, not exclusively in unreviewed appendices. |
| C05 Files/LaTeX/template | **V:** Official TACL format; LaTeX source required at publication [S1]. **R:** Download the current files from the submission portal and rebuild; do not restyle the existing generic article manually. Check embedded fonts and PDF metadata. |
| C06 Citations/references | **G:** Use the supplied ACL bibliography style [S1]. **R:** Preserve original geographical-reconstruction antecedents and distinguish API-output research from activation-based claims. Ensure every current citation resolves and avoid referring to this project as the first reconstruction of its kind. |
| C07 Figures/tables/supplement | **V:** Appendices follow references within the review PDF, remain anonymous, and are not reviewed; complementary results must be referenced from the main text [S3]. **R:** Keep the reference-sphere control and the inference caveat beside their principal figures. Consult editors before using external artifact links: older publisher guidance prohibits supplementary links, while the newer appendix policy permits replication URLs. |
| C08 Data/code/reproducibility | **G:** Release is encouraged [S1]. **V:** Promised artifacts must exist by publication [S4]. **R:** Prepare an anonymous artifact description without identifying GitHub links in the manuscript; maintain the public release separately and establish an allowed reviewer-access route. |
| C09 Methods/statistical reporting | **R:** Report the strong response-distribution null, multiplicity families, completed/valid counts, failed bootstrap calibration and observational language/wording confounding. Ten requests per pair do not establish stationary independent sampling. These are scientific adequacy checks, not a journal-mandated statistical test list. |
| C10 Human authorship/ethics/funding/conflicts | **V:** Complete coauthor profiles are required [S2]. Human authors retain responsibility [S4]. **R:** Identify actual contributors and disclose funding/conflicts privately where anonymity requires. Avoid unsupported cultural or cognitive-mechanism claims. |
| C11 Generative-AI policy/disclosure | **V:** ACL policy explicitly covers TACL; content-generation assistance needs disclosure in acknowledgements and AI cannot be an author [S4]. **R:** Audit the actual assistance with writing, code, analyses, translations and this pre-submission review. Acknowledge human development and verification truthfully; do not relabel substantive assistance as copyediting. |
| C12 Peer review/anonymity | **V:** Anonymous submissions; the former pre-submission anonymity period is removed [S2]. **R:** Remove identifying URLs and metadata, not only the currently empty author line. These commissioned AI assessments are not official TACL peer reviews. |
| C13 Fees/license/access | **V:** No submission/publication charges; immediate open access [S1]. **U:** Verify the current publication-license agreement before signing. **R:** Budget for independent language/statistics review separately; free publication does not remove scientific preparation work. |
| C14 Submission package/exclusivity/prior publication | **V:** No concurrent archival submission; nine-month exclusion after specified ACL-family conference review; twelve months after a TACL rejection [S2]. **R:** Check actual submission history, supply complete portal metadata, and retain the final submitted PDF/hash. A GitHub preprint is distinct from a refereed archival publication. |
