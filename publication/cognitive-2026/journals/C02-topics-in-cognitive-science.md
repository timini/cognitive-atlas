## Verified route and rules

The [official proposal/author guide](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/17568765/homepage/forauthors.html), marked April 2025, explicitly excludes unsolicited standalone submissions except letters or commentaries about previous topics. Suitable routes include an accepted thematic collection, emerging-field spotlight, or debate with multiple perspectives. Proposals must establish cognitive relevance and diversity, describe contributors, management, editorial experience and timetable. Individual research articles are possible within accepted topics. Topic and paper lengths are negotiated; illustrative lengths are not universal limits. Letters are approximately 400–1,000 words and commentaries 1,000–2,000, generally without abstract and with few references. The submitting author needs ORCID. Proposal and article-submission routes differ. The accessible page does not establish universal original-article abstract limits, reference format, anonymity, file format, display limits or current fees; obtain accepted-topic instructions for those details.

[Wiley's publisher policy](https://authors.wiley.com/ethics-guidelines/index.html) requires human accountability and transparent disclosure of substantive AI development, including its influence and personal verification. AI cannot be an author. Authors must protect underlying content rights and check provider terms, including training permissions. A data-availability statement is required. Editorial judgment remains decisive. These requirements do not mean the journal has pre-approved this manuscript's particular assistance history.

## Fit and tone — editorial assessment

The broader project could contribute to a collection about behavioral approaches to machine cognition, language-mediated spatial knowledge, or what fitted representational spaces do and do not establish. The current single empirical report cannot be made eligible merely by adding a thematic introduction. An invited contribution would need to connect its results to other approaches and articulate disagreements worth a shared discussion.

The useful cognitive tension is that highly accurate facts can support different fitted arrangements under different contexts. The present evidence establishes a bounded empirical case, not a general theory of language-specific worlds. Compare explanatory accounts, keep metaphors subordinate to definitions, and explain what future task-transfer or perturbation experiments could distinguish. Do not equate psychogeography with a visually distorted map alone.

No universal human-participant requirement was found. A human comparison or controlled learning study could strengthen the argument, but would be a scientific choice. Collecting more model outputs alone would not solve the submission-route restriction or supply the integrative breadth.
