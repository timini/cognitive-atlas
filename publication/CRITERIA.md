# Publication assessment protocol

Reviewed manuscript: `5f9bdaa3641f684708ad914a721eecc25835ab64`, dated 9 September 2026. Scope: 100 capitals, one Gemini 3.5 Flash alias, English/Arabic/written-Chinese instructions, ten completed sample slots per pair under each instruction condition (148,500 completed slots). All assessments concern this manuscript and its actual companion release.

These are author-requested AI-assisted submission-readiness reviews. They are not commissioned journal reports, endorsements, acceptance predictions, or independent human peer review. No journal has been contacted or received a submission. The five reviewers assess the same source snapshot independently; a different role does not imply a different underlying model or a statistically independent opinion.

Every journal guide uses the same criterion identifiers. A full identifier combines the journal and criterion, for example `J13-C08`.

## Evidence rules

Journal rules come from official journal or publisher pages checked on 9 September 2026. Guides distinguish a verified requirement, advice, and a policy we could not verify. Publisher policy applies only where the journal adopts it or belongs to the relevant portfolio. The sources can change; conflicting current pages remain unresolved rather than being silently reconciled.

Scientific fit and submission priorities are our editorial judgments, not journal requirements. An absent prescribed word limit is different from a verified absence of a limit. Prices are listed currencies on the checked page, not exchange-rate conversions or a promise of the eventual invoice. Institutional agreements, taxes and waivers require the authors' own details.

Each reviewer must cover **18 journals × 14 criteria = 252 assessments**. Compact matrices may use:

- **Pass:** the inspected artifact satisfies the applicable verified requirement; a fit pass is only reviewer judgment.
- **Partial:** there is useful evidence, but named adaptation or verification remains.
- **Fail:** an applicable requirement is demonstrably unmet, or an explicit policy conflicts with the recorded preparation process.
- **Unknown:** the current rule, eligibility, author fact, or requested evidence cannot be established.
- **N/A:** genuinely outside this study or that journal's applicable process, with an explanation.

Every matrix requires an evidence key and journal-specific reasons for non-pass cells. Do not convert an unverified policy into a pass. Do not count a production-only style change as an initial-submission rejection where format-free submission is explicitly allowed. Anonymity is assessed independently of the blank author field because public URLs and PDF metadata can identify a project.

## Scope of this work

This assessment does not change the frozen manuscript or its measurements. It identifies changes for an eventual venue-specific version. Naming human authors, attesting to contributions, declaring actual financial relationships, agreeing to publication charges, choosing a reuse license, or claiming ethics approval cannot be manufactured by a reviewer. No biomedical reporting checklist is applied merely because a broad journal publishes biomedical work.

The reproduction ZIP and GitHub repository both provide public access to analysis code and observations. Live GitHub metadata checked on 9 September 2026 reports `visibility: PUBLIC` and `isPrivate: false`; the frozen manuscript’s private-repository wording is stale. Permanent archiving and permission to reuse that code are separate questions. Likewise, studying an LLM is separate from using AI to draft, analyze, translate, code, and review the manuscript; the disclosure must describe the actual preparation history.
