## Preparation priorities for this manuscript

Prepare a concise 150-word abstract around the contrast between rank accuracy and geometric inconsistency, rather than cataloguing every available analysis. Include the model and fixed instruction conditions so the reader does not infer a survey of model families. Avoid promising that the experiment identifies a literal internal cognitive representation.

The strongest cover-letter argument is that repeated elicitation makes uncertainty visible while spherical fitting supplies a defensible global geographic comparison. Explain how the WGS 84 control separates ellipsoid–sphere mismatch from the observed residuals. Treat the website as a means of inspecting the evidence, not the main scientific novelty.

A methods reviewer should be able to reproduce every reported denominator from the archived responses. Keep the difference between available-pair descriptions, complete-case distance tests and all-pair map tests visible in the main text. The uncommon bootstrap calibration failure deserves retention rather than concealment in an inaccessible file. An explicit boundary on the language claim is more credible than describing all significant conditional tests as a general language effect.

Resolve the guideline’s contradictory upload wording at submission: one paragraph requests only a LaTeX-generated PDF initially, while later instructions describe a source-plus-PDF route. This is a portal issue, not a reason to convert the whole paper prematurely. Complete human author identities, contributions, affiliations where applicable, conflicts, funding and AI-use disclosure before upload. Deposit a durable data/code release and make its access statement consistent with what external readers can actually download.
