## Adaptation for this manuscript — editorial judgment

Resolve the policy conflict honestly before spending time on a Royal Society template. The policy is not an exemption simply because Gemini outputs are the research object: AI was also used to implement, analyze and draft this project. Human scientific ownership and an accurate preparation history must be established; ask the editorial office if this history is eligible. If that is resolved, the paper would need keywords, declarations, citation-order references, an appropriate reuse license and a flattened LaTeX submission. No actual inquiry or submission has been sent.
