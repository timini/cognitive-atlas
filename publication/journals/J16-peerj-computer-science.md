## Adaptation for this manuscript — editorial judgment

The formal DOI/software-archive requirement is more than a preference here. Deposit a human-approved licensed release in a persistent archive, or confirm the applicable supplementary-code route. Resolve the AI-policy concern before selecting PeerJ; its permission for language refinement is not evidence that assistant-created scientific drafts are accepted. If eligible, use its template, change references to author–year, separate section headings and reduce/split the downloadable archive for submission limits. Official pages returned 403 in the search browser but were successfully read by direct HTTPS; pricing remained unreadable because its content is script-rendered.
