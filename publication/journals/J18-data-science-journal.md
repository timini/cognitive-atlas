## Adaptation for this manuscript — editorial judgment

An archive DOI is an explicit criterion, not merely our recommendation. The study legitimately measures model outputs; it does not fabricate scientific observations. Nonetheless, the publisher policy uses broad wording about AI-generated original data, so authors should explain the empirical object and seek clarification rather than asserting an exception that is not documented. Reframe around reliable collection, provenance, uncertainty, and reusable benchmarking while retaining the actual geographic result. Resolve the conflicting fee quotations and the current policy version before budgeting or submission. Change references to Harvard and prepare accessible figures if the journal is chosen.
