## Preparation priorities for this manuscript

A submission here would need more than a change of vocabulary. The journal emphasizes geographically informed evidence with a real application or policy audience. This paper currently measures model judgments; it does not measure effects on planning, resource allocation, travel advice or public understanding. Those would be additional studies, not deductions from the present error table.

If the authors want to publish the existing study now, a better-fitting target avoids pressure to overstate practical relevance. If an applied extension is pursued, define the intended decision, geographic population and acceptable error before collecting new evidence. The current 100-capital study can remain the benchmark foundation.

The available instructions make this a comparatively straightforward LaTeX conversion once scope is justified. Convert numeric citations to the journal’s author–year style, introduce keywords, count the abstract and main text, and provide a complete set of sources. Keep the current definitions of distance, aggregation and uncertainty intact through template conversion.

The journal has moved submission systems and expressly warns that some identity and declaration information now belongs in interface fields. Do not embed identifying acknowledgments or repository author information into the anonymized manuscript simply because an older paragraph requests a title page. Maintain a separate authoritative author/declaration record, and populate the live fields.

The AI exemption is limited to editing human-written material for style and correctness. It does not describe the substantive assistant authoring in this project. Human authors must decide and document their contributions, verify results and citations, and take responsibility for the final article. The optional OA price is a current quote, not a guarantee of a future charge; assess funder rules and institutional arrangements separately from editorial suitability.
