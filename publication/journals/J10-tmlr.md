# J10 — Transactions on Machine Learning Research

Checked 9 September 2026. **Fit judgment: one of the stronger AI-oriented possibilities, conditional on sound claims and genuine human ownership.** The paper evaluates learned-system behavior and offers interpretable structural diagnostics. TMLR's evidence-and-interest standard is a useful match; it is not an easy acceptance route. Recommended submission: original empirical research in the standard PDF format. All results remain those of the 100-capital collection, not a proposed expanded study.

**Labels:** V = verified requirement; G = journal guidance; R = our recommendation; U = unresolved. Follow first-submission rules unless a production step is explicitly stated.

| Criterion | Submission criterion and practical style guidance |
|---|---|
| C01 Scope | **G:** Includes analysis and visualization of learning-system behavior [S2]. **R:** Emphasize what consistency diagnostics reveal about elicited model outputs; do not claim to observe training representations or learning mechanisms directly. |
| C02 Contribution/article type | **V:** Accurate evidence and intelligible findings of interest to part of the ML audience are the acceptance tests [S3]. **R:** Present a focused empirical article. State the lesson beyond the atlas: high rank accuracy does not ensure a globally realizable metric. |
| C03 Word/page/abstract limits | **G:** Variable length; excessive length can delay review [S1]. **U:** No abstract ceiling verified. **R:** Target a self-contained argument near the existing main-text length after template conversion. Cut repeated caveats by locating each at its corresponding claim, without deleting qualifications. |
| C04 Structure/title/keywords | **R:** Use the supplied template's front matter, with a literal title and concise abstract. Put the exact strong null and its limitations in the main methods/results. **U:** A separate mandatory keyword count was not verified. |
| C05 Files/LaTeX/template | **V:** Standard submissions use the mandatory TMLR LaTeX template; altered formatting risks desk rejection [S1, S4]. **R:** Use the standard PDF route rather than introducing an interactive-publication conversion at this stage. Verify the produced PDF, not just source syntax. |
| C06 Citations/references | **R:** Use the template bibliography configuration and accurate primary references. Connect the empirical result to spatial-knowledge and model-evaluation work. Do not characterize earlier numerical-distance embeddings as if this study originated that approach. **U:** No separate reference-count ceiling verified. |
| C07 Figures/tables/supplement | **V:** Appendix after references; anonymized PDF/ZIP supplementary material up to 100 MB; review of supplements is discretionary [S1]. **R:** Provide a compact anonymous reproduction bundle and keep the substantive reference control, invalid-response treatment and failed calibration in the paper. |
| C08 Data/code/reproducibility | **G:** Reproducibility materials encouraged [S1]. **R:** Preserve the input lock, all raw responses and exact prompts. Include a clear local verification command and distinguish recomputation from provider-level experimental replication. Test the anonymous package independently before uploading it. |
| C09 Methods/statistical reporting | **G:** A claim-evidence gap can be addressed by reducing claims as well as more experiments [S3]. **R:** Keep response-distribution exchangeability separate from equal-median claims, and disclose finite-support bootstrap undercoverage. Do not interpret shared-city pairs as independent country samples. |
| C10 Human authorship/ethics/funding/conflicts | **V:** Active author profiles, conflicts, funding and human-subjects information required; author set fixed at submission [S1, S2]. **R:** Complete real human author/contribution declarations before uploading. Broader-impact discussion should address misinterpretation of cultural/geographic labels without inventing human-participant ethics approval. |
| C11 Generative-AI policy/disclosure | **V:** Editorial policy permits assistive LLM use under author responsibility and prohibits AI authors [S2]. **Policy tension:** FAQ also requests a first-page disclosure footnote and human-sourced ideas/claims/results [S5]. **R:** Satisfy the explicit disclosure instruction and seek editorial clarification of extensive research assistance; never assume silence means eligibility. |
| C12 Peer review/anonymity | **V:** Double-blind OpenReview process; anonymize submission/supplements [S1, S2]. **G:** Public non-anonymous preprints allowed, but do not link the anonymous submission to them [S1]. **R:** Remove identifying GitHub links and bundle metadata. These preparatory AI assessments do not constitute TMLR peer review. |
| C13 Fees/license/access | **V:** No author fees [S2]; submissions are CC BY 4.0 from submission, with author copyright [S1]. **R:** Confirm rights before uploading. Public review and durable submission visibility should be a deliberate decision by the actual authors. |
| C14 Submission package/exclusivity/prior publication | **V:** No overlap with archival peer-reviewed work, including expanded conference papers; nonarchival/preprint overlap allowed [S2]. All authors need remaining submission quota [S2]. **R:** Check profiles, eligibility and exact author list; upload a fully prepared anonymous package through OpenReview. |
