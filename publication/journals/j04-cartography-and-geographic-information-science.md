## Preparation priorities for this manuscript

Lead with the distinction between what the model supplied and what the cartographer constructed. The measured objects are repeated numerical judgments and capital configurations fitted to those judgments. The coastlines are interpolated display geometry. A country’s outline was never queried, and a deformed shape cannot be treated as evidence that the model represents that shape internally.

The current figures already supply useful reference and residual information. A targeted revision could make units, globe-to-plane projection, deformation strength and interpolation assumptions even more prominent. Include the true-Earth fitting control in the same visual grammar as the model fits. Avoid maps whose cropping or color encodings make a small displacement look like a continent-scale cognitive change.

For this journal, the scientific argument should explain the consequences of choosing a reconstruction and map-deformation method. A visual sensitivity comparison would be useful if drawn from the actual saved coordinates, but should not be represented as a controlled user study. The paper can discuss interpretability without claiming to have measured comprehension or discovery performance.

The principal submission blocker is eligibility arising from the manuscript’s AI drafting history. The source audit documents the exact publisher wording and permitted research/visualization cases. If the editor confirms a route, then retrieve the blocked instructions and adapt the format, caption conventions and bibliography. Treat anonymity and prior dissemination as distinct checks; no journal-specific blanket preprint ban was verified for CaGIS.
