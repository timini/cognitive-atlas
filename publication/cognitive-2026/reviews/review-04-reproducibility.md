## Scientific and submission findings

The strongest current result is a reproducibly described separation of accuracy, metric consistency and repeated-response variation. The introduction explicitly defines a cognitive map as observable spatial judgments and admits fact retrieval, heuristics and internal computation as compatible explanations. This is appropriate restraint. But the title, opening question, contribution paragraph and conclusion still center geographic measurement. The explicit exploratory status means that no prospective cognitive hypotheses have been tested. A new cognitive framing is necessary work, not something accomplished by the present safe definition.

Prioritize a prediction that a fitted relational account can lose. Fit numeric relations on a training subset, then compare held-out ordinal or directional judgments against a prespecified factual or heuristic baseline. Use independently reviewed prompt variants, separately randomized collection blocks and explicit entity-name factors. Human comparisons would license claims about shared human biases; internal measurements or manipulations would license more specific mechanistic claims. Neither is a universal submission requirement. Psychogeography would need a substantive construct concerning experienced places; warped coastlines alone supply none. More model families would improve generality and expose model-by-condition variation, but repeating identical descriptive measurements cannot resolve the representation-versus-retrieval explanation gap.

The numerical safeguards are substantial. Saved whole-response validation, fixed raw records, hashes, full prompts, analysis code, null draws and multistart diagnostics make present computations inspectable. The 100-capital collection used unconstrained text, not a provider-enforced output schema; later schema implementations are excluded. The reported accounting is 148,500 completed slots, 148,504 attempts and 148,491 valid values. Complete-pair tests use 4,948 pairs, while the map permutation retains all 4,950 with observed valid counts. Invalid content is retained as evidence and is not retried until acceptable.

I ran the read-only paper verifier successfully and the seven targeted paper tests passed. The public reproduction ZIP contains 140 members, passes CRC integrity checks and matches its published SHA-256. These are useful local release checks, not a clean-environment full rebuild, a rerun of the expensive map audit, independent parser replay in this review, or fresh provider observations. The saved QC report records zero parser-replay mismatches. The normal build guards expected input/release hashes, and the documented analysis path requires no model calls. The current release can therefore be checked much more fully than its historical execution can be reconstructed: dirty-source state and per-resumed-session collector snapshots are missing; historical identities omitted coastline/runtime inputs; the measured model is an alias rather than a frozen checkpoint. Later hashes cannot retroactively supply those records.

Do not rehabilitate the old inferential claims while making the paper sound more cognitive. Error-sign controls preserve each pair’s absolute error but give only descriptive artificial-reference ranges. Conditional permutations address equal response distributions under exchangeability, not equal population median maps or a language-only effect. Regional gains show no observed absolute advantage; severe rare-tail bootstrap undercoverage prevents reliable significance claims. These corrections strengthen integrity while reducing the force of a headline about uniquely incoherent or culturally determined model worlds.

The remaining publication barriers are real author identity, intellectual accountability, verified declarations, reuse licenses and permanent archiving. Public downloadability is not permission to reuse. A Natural Earth source license does not establish rights to the entire project or its AI-assisted material. Preserve every recorded drafting, analysis and translation role, acknowledge unknown historical assistant versions, and distinguish the measured Gemini system from preparation tools. Another agent review cannot supply human approval. Anonymity requires a separate review-accessible materials route at venues with double-anonymous review; making the project private or pretending it has not been posted would not solve the record.

I rechecked the current official [Taylor & Francis AI guidance](https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/editorial-policies/using-ai-in-your-research-and-manuscript-preparations/), [Elsevier AI policy](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals) and [Springer journal policy](https://link.springer.com/brands/springer/journal-policies). The Taylor & Francis first-draft restriction creates an actual eligibility hold for C09; clarification must report the true history. Disclosed assistance at other publishers still requires accountable human scholarly judgment. Missing official journal-specific requirements remain Unknown rather than being inferred from another journal. Fees are preparation information, not permission to incur charges.

- **C09 — Spatial Cognition & Computation:** Direct topical match with an unresolved drafting-policy barrier. Seek clarification on the true history before treating this as eligible; even clearance would leave predictive validation and format checks outstanding.
- **C10 — Cognitive Processing:** Best practical cognitive route in this review, after substantive argument and validation work. Its artificial-cognition scope fits, but abstract, references, anonymity, authorship and rights are not ready.
- **C11 — Journal of Environmental Psychology:** Weak current fit. No measured human–environment construct supports the environmental-psychology bridge; a specific psychological comparison or computational theory would require new substantive work.
- **C12 — Frontiers in Psychology — Cognition:** Conditional Original Research route. The current benchmark needs a tested psychological foundation, plus scope statement, supplements and verified human responsibility; more models alone do not resolve scope.
