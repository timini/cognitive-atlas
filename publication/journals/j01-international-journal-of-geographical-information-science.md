## Preparation priorities for this manuscript

Treat eligibility separately from scientific merit. The public manuscript links to an account-associated project, and the authoring history includes substantive assistant-generated prose. Neither issue should be hidden in a cover letter or described as ordinary proofreading. An editor may decide the paper is ineligible regardless of format.

If eligibility is established, sharpen the GIScience contribution around what repeated judgments reveal beyond pairwise accuracy: triangle violations, spherical compatibility, sampling variation and geometry estimated from the complete relation matrix. The WGS 84 spherical reference is a useful control because it quantifies representation mismatch without assigning all distortion to the model. Keep that control prominent.

The language experiment remains a comparison of three fixed instruction conditions. A full factorial linguistic claim would need independently checked translations and replicated phrasings. Do not add such claims to improve perceived novelty. Shorten implementation detail that does not change scientific interpretation, while preserving exact prompts, hashes and optimizer records in the supplement.

Before formatting, inspect the live regular-article instructions rather than adopting the special-issue deadline or invitation procedure. This guide uses that publisher-hosted call only where it expressly states journal-wide rules. Obtain the missing numerical limits and reference/template requirements; unverified cells are not passes.
