## Verified scope and theory bar

The [Cognition section](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/sections/cognition/about) accepts AI and computational approaches when they advance understanding of human cognition. It excludes work without a strong cognitive-process emphasis or psychological theoretical foundation. The [journal scope](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/about) likewise excludes purely technological AI work lacking psychological foundations. Review is single anonymized: reviewers see authors; reviewer identities are initially protected and appear on published articles. Articles use CC-BY and authors retain copyright.

## Verified article and style requirements

[Original Research](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/for-authors/article-types) permits 12,000 main-body words and follows abstract, introduction, methods, results, discussion. Brief Research Reports allow 4,000 words and four display items. The current eight-display article fits the longer route better. A Data Report is not a shortcut for the current results paper.

The [author guide](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/for-authors/author-guidelines) requires 5–8 keywords, page and line numbering, single spacing and first-page word/figure/table counts. Word and LaTeX are supported. Abstracts should communicate conceptual significance in one paragraph; a numerical abstract ceiling was not verified. Submission needs a 200-word scope statement. Appendices longer than one page belong in supplements. Figures are supplied separately with accessible descriptions. The exact Psychology reference-style mapping remains unverified because its linked help page rendered only a shell; do not treat the generic Harvard/Vancouver alternatives as a journal-specific choice. AI-written or edited content must be checked and acknowledged, giving technology name, version, model and source; include Methods disclosure when relevant. AI cannot be an author.

The [data policy](https://www.frontiersin.org/guidelines/policies-and-publication-ethics) requires a data-availability statement and access to the minimal supporting dataset. Authors must provide raw data when requested, document contributions and disclose conflicts. Human-study ethics apply if human research is actually conducted. All human author approvals and declarations remain outstanding facts, not statements for an assistant to invent. The [APC table](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/for-authors/publishing-fees) currently lists CHF 3,150 for Original Research and CHF 2,500 for Brief Research Reports; check support arrangements and the live quote before submission.

## Editorial tone — assessment, not publisher rules

Do not equate a section named Cognition with automatic acceptance of a model benchmark. The present article could make a stronger case by treating language-derived spatial organization as a comparative cognitive phenomenon and testing a theoretically motivated prediction about human cognition. A human dataset is one possible bridge, not an invented blanket requirement. Another is a falsifiable computational account tested against existing cognitive evidence with appropriate validation.

The current introduction defines a safe operational map but underdevelops why its properties matter to cognitive theory. Replace the defensive sequence of caveats with a positive question about relational knowledge, then place each caveat beside the inference it limits. The discussion should explain which accounts the data constrain and which remain compatible. A map illustration can support this argument, but cannot stand in for it.

Additional model families would establish the breadth of a pattern. They will not alone satisfy the psychological basis requirement. Prioritize cross-task transfer, independently checked prompt variants and theory-based distortion predictions. The current Arabic/English difference does not isolate language, and the regional findings do not demonstrate local-language advantage.
