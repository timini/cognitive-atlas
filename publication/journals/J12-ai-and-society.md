# J12 — AI & SOCIETY

Checked 9 September 2026. **Fit judgment: low priority and significant policy concern for this project.** A socially grounded argument could connect to the journal, but the present measurements do not establish cultural harms, colonial effects, or language-community disadvantage. Adding those interpretations without evidence would weaken the paper. Its journal-specific policy also strongly discourages LLM use beyond grammar/translation. The extensive assistant involvement here therefore requires candid editorial clarification, not a cosmetic disclosure. Recommended category if pursued: Research Article; not an opinion category as a workaround.

**Labels:** V = verified rule; G = journal guidance; R = our recommendation; U = unresolved. The journal warns that its new submission system changes where author declarations are entered; follow its actual interface.

| Criterion | Submission criterion and practical style guidance |
|---|---|
| C01 Scope | **G:** Societal, cultural and philosophical implications of technology are central [S1]. **R:** Explain a supported societal research question. A geographic error map alone does not demonstrate social bias, and a regional proxy is not a measure of language-community experience. |
| C02 Contribution/article type | **G:** Research Articles require an explicit societal argument [S1]. **R:** The current paper needs intellectual reframing and relevant evidence, not just fewer equations. Retain the fixed-condition limitations rather than generalizing from one API collection to global AI culture. |
| C03 Word/page/abstract limits | **G:** Normal Research length 10,000 words [S1]. **V:** Abstract normally 150–250 words; specified reporting needs may justify up to 450 [S2]. **R:** Use the ordinary limit here. Do not expand merely to approach the normal article length. |
| C04 Structure/title/keywords | **V:** Four to six keywords; decimal headings, maximum three levels [S2]. **R:** Use accessible section titles, define geodesic and Procrustes terms, and lead with the empirical question. Keep a clear separation between observation and speculative social interpretation. |
| C05 Files/LaTeX/template | **V:** Journal-specific instructions say Word only [S2]. **R:** Convert to editable DOCX and inspect every equation, citation and map. Generic text elsewhere mentioning LaTeX does not override the explicit Word-only note. Retain LaTeX as the research source. |
| C06 Citations/references | **V:** Author–year citations; alphabetized bibliography; DOI links where available [S2]. **R:** Do not add superficial social-science citations to manufacture fit. Engage critically with scholarship directly relevant to a claim the data can address. |
| C07 Figures/tables/supplement | **V:** Number and cite tables; caption supplements as Online Resources [S2]. **R:** Reproduce the map axes, controls and uncertainty explanations in static form. Supply editable charts or high-quality vector artwork and verify final-size legibility. The website cannot replace reviewed evidence. |
| C08 Data/code/reproducibility | **V:** Original research requires a Data Availability Statement; data sharing encouraged [S2]. **R:** Link the archived current dataset and code, preserving anonymous reviewer access. Ensure licensing and third-party map attribution are clear while checking that public project links do not compromise the anonymous review package. |
| C09 Methods/statistical reporting | **R:** Explain distributions, effect sizes and the conditional null in accessible prose. Preserve the failed bootstrap diagnostic and distinguish language from wording. Establish an appropriate unit of inference before connecting city-pair findings to societies or populations. No journal-specific mandatory test family was verified. |
| C10 Human authorship/ethics/funding/conflicts | **V:** Contribution and competing-interest information entered in the submission interface [S2]. **R:** Record actual human contributions, funding and relevant interests. State the study's nonhuman design without inventing ethics approval. Disclose assistance honestly rather than treating agent checks as human peer review. |
| C11 Generative-AI policy/disclosure | **G:** Journal strongly discourages LLM use beyond grammar/translation [S1]. **V:** Disclose substantive LLM use in Methods; AI cannot author [S2]. **R:** Seek clarification before submission, with the full assistance history. Disclosure does not itself resolve compatibility with this journal-specific discouragement. |
| C12 Peer review/anonymity | **V:** Double-blind; anonymize all manuscript materials [S2]. **R:** Remove identifying links and metadata from the review version while entering genuine author information privately in the portal. Reconcile the old separate-title-page language with the new system instructions. |
| C13 Fees/license/access | **V:** Hybrid: no APC for subscription; current optional OA £2,390/$3,390/€2,790 plus applicable taxes, price fixed at acceptance. OA licenses CC BY or CC BY-NC-ND [S3]. **R:** Check funding eligibility and current quote before selecting OA; no payment is authorized by this guide. |
| C14 Submission package/exclusivity/prior publication | **V:** Exclusive submission; acknowledge academic-site pre-publications [S2]. **R:** Declare the public paper/atlas accurately and prepare Word, figures, statements and author records. Confirm policy compatibility before investing in full conversion. This journal is a reserve candidate, not a recommendation to submit unchanged. |
